## You will be able to
Choose between the four basic filter shapes by what each one keeps and what it removes.

## Hear it first
One note through four filters: low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, notch.

## The idea
The same cutoff control can do four different jobs. Low-pass keeps what is below it. High-pass keeps what is above it. Band-pass keeps a slice around it. Notch removes a slice around it and keeps the rest. On the band-pass and notch shapes, resonance sets how narrow the slice is.

## Steps
| Do | On screen | Check | You should hear |
|---|---|---|---|
| Switch on Hold and play a note on the low-pass filter. | Filter type, Filter curve | Low-pass, note held | The rounded tone from the last two lessons. |
| Switch to High-pass. | Filter type, Filter curve | High-pass selected | Thin and buzzy: the body is gone and the top is left. The curve has flipped. |
| Raise the cutoff until only fizz is left. | Cutoff, Filter curve | High-pass with the cutoff at 3 kHz or above | The note’s weight draining away from the bottom up. |
| Switch to Band-pass and bring the cutoff back to around 1 kHz. | Filter type, Cutoff, Filter curve | Band-pass with the cutoff between 700 Hz and 1.5 kHz | A small, boxy sound, like a voice on a telephone. |
| Raise Resonance to narrow the band. | Resonance, Filter curve | Band-pass with resonance at 50% or more | The slice closing in toward a single whistling harmonic. |
| Switch to Notch and sweep the cutoff. | Filter type, Cutoff, Resonance, Filter curve | Notch, with the cutoff swept across two octaves | A subtle hollow spot moving through the sound. |

## In Serum
On Filter 1, open the filter type menu and try a high-pass, a band-pass and a notch type while sweeping CUTOFF. Listening only: Serum has many more types than these four.

In the guide: filter types, pp. 135-139. Serum has many more types than the four here.

## Recap
- Low-pass keeps the lows; high-pass keeps the highs.
- Band-pass keeps a slice; notch removes one.
- On band-pass and notch, resonance sets the width of the slice.

## Guide notes
The notch is subtle on a static note; tell the learner that is expected and to listen while it moves.
