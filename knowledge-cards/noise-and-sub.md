## What it is
Beside the main oscillators there are two helpers. The noise oscillator adds unpitched hiss or a short recorded texture. The sub oscillator adds a simple, low tone under the main pitch.

## Why it works
Noise contains energy spread across many frequencies with no single pitch, so it reads as breath, air, grit or the click at the start of a note. The sub is the opposite: almost all of its energy is at one low frequency, which gives a solid foundation that stays steady even when the main oscillators are detuned, filtered or distorted.

## Listen for
Noise shows up as a constant texture on held notes or a burst at the start. The sub is felt more than heard on small speakers; on headphones the low end should feel anchored.

## Goes with
Subs are often routed around the filter and effects so they stay clean. Noise is usually shaped by its own level envelope or kept very quiet.
