## You will be able to
Make the classic moving-filter bass and control its depth, speed and character.

## Hear it first
A dark bass note, then the same note with an LFO opening and closing the filter.

## The idea
Send an LFO to the cutoff and the filter opens and closes by itself. With some resonance, each sweep has a vowel-like “wow” to it. This is the wobble bass, and slower versions of the same thing are how pads are made to breathe. Three settings carry the character. Amount is how far the cutoff swings. Rate is how fast. Shape is whether it glides, snaps or ramps. The cutoff knob sets the middle of the swing.

## Steps
| Do | On screen | Check | You should hear |
|---|---|---|---|
| Switch on Hold and play a low note. The filter is on, dark, with some resonance. | LFO 1 sends to, Cutoff | LFO 1 sent to nothing, note held | A dull, steady bass note. |
| Send LFO 1 to Filter cutoff. | LFO 1 sends to, LFO 1 amount, Cutoff, Filter curve | LFO 1 sent to Filter cutoff | A gentle “wow-wow”. Watch the filter curve move, and the cyan ring on Cutoff. |
| Raise Amount to about 60%. | LFO 1 amount, Cutoff, Filter curve | Amount between 50% and 75% | A much deeper sweep. |
| Set Rate to about 3 cycles a second. | LFO 1 rate, LFO 1 amount, Filter curve | Rate between 2.5 and 4 Hz | A steady “wub wub wub”. |
| Try each Shape and finish on Saw. | LFO 1 shape, Filter curve | Shape changed at least twice, now on Saw | Square flips between two filter positions. Saw gives each wub a rising sweep and a snap shut. |
| While it wobbles, turn the Cutoff knob up and down. | Cutoff, Filter curve | Cutoff moved across at least an octave | The whole wobble moving brighter and darker: you are shifting the middle of the swing. |

## In Serum
From Init, switch on Filter 1, lower CUTOFF, and drag LFO 1 onto the CUTOFF knob. Save it and drop it into the “LFOs and modulation” Serum task to see what the app can read from it.

In the guide: LFOs, from p. 187; filter CUTOFF, p. 141.

## Recap
- An LFO on the cutoff opens and closes the filter by itself.
- Amount is depth, rate is speed, shape is character.
- The cutoff knob sets the middle of the swing.

## Guide notes
Report the swing in octaves. Point at the cyan ring and the moving filter curve.
