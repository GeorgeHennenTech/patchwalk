## What it is
Delay records the sound and plays it back a moment later. Feedback sends that repeat back into the delay so it repeats again, a little quieter each time.

## Why it works
A repeat that arrives late enough is heard as an echo, and a series of fading echoes fills the gaps between notes and suggests space. When the delay time is locked to the tempo, the echoes fall on the beat and become part of the rhythm. Different times on the left and right make the echoes bounce across the stereo field.

## Listen for
Play one short note and count the echoes. More feedback gives more of them; filtering inside the delay makes each one darker or thinner so they sit behind the dry sound.

## Goes with
The mix sets how loud the echoes are against the original. Long, loud delays quickly clutter fast playing, which is why the mix is so often under a macro.
