## What it is
Distortion pushes the signal harder than a circuit or a mathematical curve can pass cleanly, so the tops of the wave are flattened or folded.

## Why it works
Flattening a wave changes its shape, and a changed shape contains new harmonics above the original ones. Gentle settings add warmth and density; heavy settings add buzz and aggression. Because the effect depends on how loud the incoming signal is, it also evens out level differences, and when two notes or two detuned oscillators go in together it creates extra tones between them, which is why distorted chords sound thick or dirty.

## Listen for
More drive brings more edge and a fuller, louder-seeming sound. Past a point the low end smears and the tone turns fizzy.

## Goes with
What comes before it matters: a filter before distortion changes what is distorted, a filter or EQ after it tames the added highs. The mix control blends the clean signal back in to keep definition.
