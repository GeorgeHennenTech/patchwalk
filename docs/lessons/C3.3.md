## You will be able to
Keep a note at full level for a moment before it falls, and choose the shape of the fall.

## Hear it first
The same pluck falling in three shapes: straight, eased, sharp. Then with a short hold at the top.

## The idea
Two more controls finish the envelope. Hold keeps the note at full level for a set time after the attack, before the decay begins. It adds weight to the front of a note without making the whole note longer. Fall is the shape of the decay and release. Straight loses level at an even rate. Eased drops quickly at first and then slows, which is how most plucked and struck things behave. Sharp does that more strongly: most of the level is gone almost at once, with a long quiet tail. The decay time is the same in all three; what changes is where in that time the sound goes.

## Steps
| Do | On screen | Check | You should hear |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strike a few keys. This is a pluck with an eased fall. | Envelope 1 drawing, Env 1 fall | Eased fall, two notes played | A note that drops away naturally. |
| Switch Fall to Straight and strike again. | Envelope 1 drawing, Env 1 fall | Straight fall, two notes played | The note seems longer and flatter, then stops rather suddenly. |
| Switch Fall to Sharp and strike again. | Envelope 1 drawing, Env 1 fall | Sharp fall, two notes played | A hard, snappy front and a thin tail. The same decay time, but it sounds much shorter. |
| Stay on Sharp and raise Hold to about 200 ms. | Envelope 1 drawing, Env 1 hold, Env 1 fall | Sharp fall, hold between 120 and 300 ms, two notes played | A solid block of sound at the front, then the snap. Punchier, without ringing on. |
| Choose the Fall and Hold you like best for a pluck. | Envelope 1 drawing, Env 1 hold, Env 1 decay, Env 1 fall | Fall or hold changed in this step, a note played | Your choice. |

## In Serum
ENV 1 in Serum has a HOLD knob between ATK and DEC, and each stage of the envelope drawing can be dragged into a curve. Listening only.

In the guide: envelopes, from p. 183 (HOLD, and curved stages in the envelope display).

## Recap
- Hold keeps a note at full level before it starts to fall.
- The shape of the fall changes how long a note seems, at the same decay time.
- Eased and sharp falls sound struck or plucked; a straight fall sounds mechanical.

## Guide notes
The practice synth has one fall shape for decay and release together, with three settings; Serum lets each stage be bent freely. The remark's fractions come from the same maths the sound uses.
