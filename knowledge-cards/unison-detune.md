## What it is
Unison stacks several copies of the same oscillator. Detune pushes those copies slightly apart in pitch so they stop sounding like one voice.

## Why it works
Copies that are almost, but not exactly, in tune drift in and out of phase with each other. Where they line up the sound gets louder, where they oppose it thins out, and because each harmonic drifts at its own speed the tone shimmers instead of sitting still. More voices fill in the gaps between those drifts, which is why a large stack sounds smooth while a small one sounds like distinct pitches beating.

## Listen for
With no detune the stack only sounds louder. As detune rises you first hear a slow swirl, then a wide chorus-like thickness, and eventually the pitch itself turns blurry and sour. Spreading the voices across the stereo field makes the sound wider but can hollow out the centre.

## Goes with
Random start phase keeps every note from beginning with the same hard edge. Heavy detune on low notes muddies the bass, so thick stacks are usually kept above the low register or paired with a clean sub.
