## What it is
An equalizer turns chosen frequency ranges up or down. Each band has a frequency where it acts, a gain for how much, and a width.

## Why it works
Unlike the main filter, which gives a patch its character, an EQ is usually corrective or supportive: removing rumble or boxiness, or lifting a range that should stand out. Narrow cuts remove a specific ringing; broad lifts change the overall tilt. Where the EQ sits matters, because cutting lows before a distortion changes how the distortion behaves, while the same cut after it only changes the result.

## Listen for
Sweep a band's frequency with a boost to find what each range contributes, then decide whether that range needs more or less.

## Goes with
An EQ band is also a modulation target. Moving a band's frequency with an LFO or macro gives a sweeping movement that is gentler than moving the main filter.
