## What it is
This effect has two halves. Hyper layers several slightly detuned, slightly delayed copies of the sound, like unison applied after the fact. Dimension adds a short, wide ambience by mixing in delayed copies that differ between left and right.

## Why it works
Both rely on the same idea as chorus: near-identical copies that do not quite line up are heard as more sources and more space. Hyper thickens anything that reaches it, including sources that have no unison of their own. Dimension widens without a long tail, so it adds size while keeping the sound close and clear.

## Listen for
Hyper makes the sound fuller and slightly blurred, as if doubled. Dimension makes it seem to come from a wider area without sounding like a room.

## Goes with
Either one stacks with oscillator unison and chorus, so small amounts are usually enough. They are common under a macro so the width can be opened up for some sections only.
