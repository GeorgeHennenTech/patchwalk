## What it is
Each sound source has its own level. With more than one source switched on, the levels set the balance between them.

## Why it works
A patch with several sources is a small mix. The loudest source defines the character and the quieter ones support it: a sub for weight, noise for air, a second oscillator for thickness. Levels also set how hard the combined signal hits whatever follows, so a filter with drive or a distortion effect reacts differently when the sources are louder.

## Listen for
Bring one source down and notice which quality of the sound goes with it. If everything gets harsher as a level rises, something after the oscillators is being driven.

## Goes with
A source parked at zero level with a macro or LFO routed to it is not silent in practice; check the modulation before assuming it is unused.
