## What it is
The effect's own mix knob is left at zero, and a macro is routed to that mix. The effect is in the rack and fully set up, but you only hear it as far as the macro is turned.

## Why it works
Modulation adds to a knob's resting value. With the resting value at zero, the macro's position becomes the whole mix amount, so one labelled knob on the front panel replaces a trip into the effects page. The saved position of the macro is therefore part of the sound: the patch loads with exactly that much effect.

## Listen for
Turn the macro down and the effect should disappear completely; turn it up and it fades in smoothly. If you copy the effect's settings but forget the macro route, the patch will sound dry no matter what the effect's other knobs say.

## Goes with
This is how preset makers give a patch a consistent set of performance controls. It also means the mix knobs in the rack are misleading on their own: always check what is routed to them.
