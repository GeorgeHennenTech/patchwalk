## What it is
A handful of settings apply to the patch as a whole: the master level, how far the pitch-bend wheel moves the pitch in each direction, and how notes are allocated to voices.

## Why it works
These do not shape the tone so much as decide how the patch behaves under your hands. The bend range turns the wheel into either a subtle expressive control or a dramatic dive. The master level sets how hot the patch arrives in your mix and, for patches with effects that react to level, can change the character. Voice settings decide what happens when the same note is struck repeatedly or many notes are held.

## Listen for
Move the pitch-bend wheel fully each way and note how far the pitch travels. Compare the patch's loudness with others at the same fader position.

## Goes with
A saved mod wheel position is controller state, not sound design, though it matters when the wheel is routed to something.
