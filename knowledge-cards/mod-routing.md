## What it is
A modulation route connects a source that produces movement, such as an envelope, LFO, macro or the mod wheel, to a destination setting. The route's amount says how far the source may push the destination.

## Why it works
The destination's knob sets a resting value and the route adds movement on top of it. A one-way route pushes the setting in one direction from its resting value; a bipolar route swings it both above and below. Because the result is the knob plus the modulation, the knob position alone no longer tells you what you hear, and a small amount on a sensitive destination can matter more than a large amount on a dull one.

## Listen for
Set the amount to nothing and the movement stops; restore it and the movement returns. A negative amount turns the movement upside down.

## Goes with
A second source can be set to scale a route, which turns that source into a depth control. Several routes may share one destination, and their effects add together.
