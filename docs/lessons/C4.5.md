## You will be able to
Choose whether an LFO starts again with every note or keeps running, and make its movement fade in.

## Hear it first
A vibrato that is there from the start of the note, then one that fades in after the note has begun.

## The idea
An LFO set to Restart begins its shape again every time you strike a key, so every note moves the same way. Set to Free, it runs on regardless of your playing, and each note catches it wherever it happens to be. Restart is predictable, which suits basses and anything rhythmic. Free is varied, which suits pads and anything that should not sound mechanical. Rise is a separate idea: a fade-in for the LFO’s effect. With a rise, each note starts steady and the movement grows in. Singers and string players do this with vibrato on long notes.

## Steps
| Do | On screen | Check | You should hear |
|---|---|---|---|
| LFO 1 is a slow ramp on the filter, set to Restart. Strike the same key four or five times, not in any rhythm. | LFO 1 start, Filter curve | Restart, four notes played | Every note begins the same way: dark, then opening. |
| Switch Start to Free and strike the key four or five times again. | LFO 1 start, Filter curve | Free, four notes played | Each note begins somewhere different: some bright, some dark. |
| Go back to Restart. Send LFO 1 to Osc A pitch instead, with Shape on Sine, Rate about 6 and Amount at 3%. | LFO 1 start, LFO 1 sends to, LFO 1 shape, LFO 1 rate, LFO 1 amount | Restart, sent to Osc A pitch, sine, rate 5 to 8 Hz, amount 1% to 4% | A vibrato that is on from the first instant of every note. |
| Raise Rise to about one second, then hold a key for a few seconds. | LFO 1 rise, LFO 1 amount | Rise between 700 ms and 1.5 s, a note held for two seconds | A steady note at first, with the vibrato growing in. |
| Play a mix of short and long notes. | LFO 1 rise | Four notes played, one of them held for a second and a half | Short notes plain, long notes singing. |

## In Serum
Serum’s LFO has three modes: RETRIG, FREE and ENVELOPE (guide p. 191), and RISE and DELAY knobs. ENVELOPE, where the shape plays once and stops, is not in the practice synth yet.

In the guide: LFO modes, p. 191 (FREE, RETRIG, ENVELOPE); RISE and DELAY.

## Recap
- Restart begins the LFO again with every note; Free lets it run on.
- Restart is consistent; Free is varied.
- Rise fades the movement in, so long notes get more of it than short ones.

## Guide notes
Notes are struck, not held with Hold, so the start of each note can be compared. Envelope mode and Delay are not built.
