## You will be able to
Make the filter follow the keyboard so low and high notes are equally bright.

## Hear it first
A low and a high note through a fixed filter, then the same two notes with the filter following the keys.

## The idea
A cutoff is a fixed frequency, but notes are not. A cutoff that leaves a low note bright can sit below a high note’s harmonics and leave it dull. Key tracking moves the cutoff with the key you play. At 100% it moves exactly as far as the pitch does, so every note keeps the same number of harmonics. Here it pivots on middle C: that key always gets the cutoff you set.

## Steps
| Do | On screen | Check | You should hear |
|---|---|---|---|
| With Key track at 0, play a low key and then a high key. | Key track, Filter curve | Key track at 0, two different keys played | The low note bright, the high note dull: the cutoff stays put while the note moves past it. |
| Set Key track to 100%. | Key track, Filter curve | Key track at 100% | Nothing until you play. |
| Play the same low and high keys again and watch the curve. | Key track, Filter curve | Key track at 100%, two different keys played | Both notes equally bright. The curve jumps to a new place for each key. |
| Set Key track to about 50% and play low and high again. | Key track, Filter curve | Key track between 40% and 60%, two different keys played | A halfway house: high notes a little softer than low ones. |
| Choose the amount that sounds most even to you across the keyboard. | Key track, Filter curve | Key track moved in this step, two different keys played | Your choice. |

## In Serum
Filter 1 has a keytrack switch beside CUTOFF. The manual says that with it on, one octave on the keyboard moves the cutoff one octave on most filter types. Serum’s is an on/off switch, not an amount. Play low and high notes with it on and off. Listening only.

In the guide: Cutoff, p. 141: the keytrack switch. Serum's is on/off; the practice synth has an amount.

## Recap
- A fixed cutoff makes high notes duller than low ones.
- Key tracking moves the cutoff with the key.
- At 100% every note keeps the same brightness.

## Guide notes
Tell the learner where the cutoff actually sat on their last key. Key tracking here pivots on middle C.
