## You will be able to
Tell an LFO on level from an LFO on pitch, and run two LFOs at once.

## Hear it first
Tremolo, where the level moves; then vibrato, where the pitch moves.

## The idea
The same LFO gives two classic effects depending on where it is sent. On level it is tremolo: the note pulses louder and quieter. On pitch it is vibrato: the note wavers sharp and flat, the way a singer or violinist does. Pitch is far more sensitive than level. A tremolo needs a large amount to be heard; a vibrato needs a tiny one, and more than that turns it into a siren.

## Steps
| Do | On screen | Check | You should hear |
|---|---|---|---|
| Switch on Hold and play a note. Send LFO 1 to Osc A level and raise its Amount to about 60%. | LFO 1 sends to, LFO 1 amount, A level | LFO 1 sent to Osc A level, amount 50% or more, note held | The note pulsing: tremolo. |
| Set Rate to about 6 cycles a second. | LFO 1 rate, LFO 1 amount | Rate between 5 and 8 Hz | The speed of a classic guitar-amp tremolo. |
| Now send LFO 1 to Osc A pitch, and bring Amount right down to 2 or 3%. Click the knob and use the arrow keys for small steps. | LFO 1 sends to, LFO 1 amount, Fine | LFO 1 sent to Osc A pitch, amount between 1% and 4% | A gentle waver in pitch: vibrato. |
| Raise Amount to 40% or more. | LFO 1 amount, Fine | Amount at 40% or more | A siren: the pitch is now sweeping across several notes. |
| Bring LFO 1 back to a small vibrato. Then send LFO 2 to Osc A level as well. | LFO 1 amount, LFO 2 sends to, LFO 2 amount, LFO 2 rate, Fine, A level | LFO 1 on pitch at 1% to 4%, LFO 2 sent to Osc A level | Vibrato and tremolo together, at different speeds. Two knobs now wear rings: cyan for LFO 1, green for LFO 2. |

## In Serum
Drag LFO 1 onto an oscillator’s LEVEL for tremolo, or onto its FIN (fine pitch) for vibrato, and keep the amount small. Listening only: how the amount reads on screen is not confirmed.

In the guide: oscillator pitch and level controls, pp. 40-49.

## Recap
- An LFO on level is tremolo; on pitch it is vibrato.
- Pitch needs a tiny amount; level needs a large one.
- Several LFOs can run at once, each on its own control and at its own speed.

## Guide notes
Do the cents arithmetic. Small amounts need the arrow keys. Introduces a second LFO and its green ring.
