## You will be able to
Switch on a low-pass filter and use its cutoff to take brightness off a sound, from the top down.

## Hear it first
The same bright note three times: untouched, with the top taken off, then with most of it gone.

## The idea
A filter turns down part of a sound and leaves the rest alone. A low-pass filter lets the low part pass and turns down everything above one frequency, the cutoff. You already know a bright sound is a long row of harmonics. Lowering the cutoff removes them from the top, one after another, so the same note goes from buzzy to rounded to dull. The curve on screen shows what gets through: flat on the left, falling away on the right.

## Steps
| Do | On screen | Check | You should hear |
|---|---|---|---|
| Switch on Hold and play a note with the filter off. | Filter, Spectrum | Filter off, note held | The plain bright tone, with its full row of peaks. |
| Switch the filter on. Its cutoff starts all the way up. | Filter, Filter curve | Filter on | No change yet: the cutoff is above everything in the sound. |
| Bring Cutoff down slowly to about 1 kHz. | Cutoff, Filter curve | Cutoff between 800 Hz and 1.2 kHz | The buzz fading from the top down. |
| Keep going until only a dull hum is left. | Cutoff, Filter curve | Cutoff at 250 Hz or below | Almost a pure tone: only the lowest harmonics survive. |
| Now grab the dot on the filter curve and sweep it left and right a few times. | Filter curve | The dot dragged across at least two octaves | The tone opening and closing, like a mouth saying “wow”. |
| Settle on a cutoff where the sound is rounded but not dull. | Cutoff, Filter curve | Cutoff between 400 Hz and 4 kHz, moved in this step | Your choice: softer than the raw tone, still clear. |

## In Serum
From Init, switch on Filter 1 and bring CUTOFF down. Save it and drop it into “The filter” Serum task: the app checks that the filter is on and the cutoff has moved.

In the guide: the filter module and its CUTOFF knob, pp. 133-143 (cutoff on p. 141).

## Recap
- A low-pass filter passes the lows and turns down what is above the cutoff.
- Lowering the cutoff removes harmonics from the top down.
- The filter curve shows what gets through at each frequency.

## Guide notes
Say how many harmonics of the held note sit below the cutoff. A common mistake is expecting a change the moment the filter is switched on: the cutoff starts fully open.
