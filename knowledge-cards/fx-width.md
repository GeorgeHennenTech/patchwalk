## What it is
A utility stage at the end of the chain handles plain housekeeping: overall level, pan, and how wide the sound is across the left and right speakers.

## Why it works
Width comes from the difference between the two channels. Raising it exaggerates that difference so the sound seems to spread beyond the speakers; lowering it folds everything toward the middle, and at the bottom the sound is mono. Wide sounds feel big on their own but can lose power when the two sides partly cancel on a single speaker.

## Listen for
At low width the sound sits as a point in the centre. As width rises it opens outward. If the sound gets quieter or hollow when summed to mono, it is too wide.

## Goes with
Width only has something to work with if earlier stages created a difference between left and right, such as unison spread, chorus or a stereo delay. Bass content is usually kept narrow.
