## You will be able to
Shape how a note’s loudness starts, settles and ends, using the four stages of an envelope.

## Hear it first
The same note three ways: switched straight on and off, fading in slowly, then ringing on after it is let go.

## The idea
So far every note has switched on and off like a light. An envelope gives a note a shape in time. Attack is how long it takes to reach full level. Decay is how long it then takes to fall to the sustain level. Sustain is the level it stays at while you hold the key. Release is how long it takes to fade after you let go. Three of the four are times; sustain is a level. Envelope 1 on this synth always controls loudness. The drawing shows the shape, and you can drag its points.

## Steps
| Do | On screen | Check | You should hear |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strike a few keys, some short and some long. Do not use Hold in this lesson. | Envelope 1 drawing | Three notes played | Notes that start and stop at once, like a switch. |
| Raise Attack to about half a second, then strike a key and hold it. | Envelope 1 drawing, Env 1 attack | Attack between 400 and 700 ms, a note played | The note fading in. |
| Bring Attack back down to almost nothing. Set Sustain to 0 and Decay to about 300 ms, then hold a key. | Envelope 1 drawing, Env 1 attack, Env 1 decay, Env 1 sustain | Attack 20 ms or less, sustain 0, decay 200 to 500 ms, a note played | A pluck: the note dies away though you are still holding the key. |
| Raise Sustain to about 50% and hold a key for a couple of seconds. | Envelope 1 drawing, Env 1 decay, Env 1 sustain | Sustain between 40% and 60%, a note held for two seconds | A strong start that drops, then stays at a lower level for as long as you hold. |
| Raise Release to about one second and play a few short notes. | Envelope 1 drawing, Env 1 release | Release between 700 ms and 1.5 s, two notes played | Each note ringing on after you let go, overlapping the next. |
| Now drag the points on the drawing itself to make a shape of your own, and play it. | Envelope 1 drawing, Env 1 attack, Env 1 decay, Env 1 sustain, Env 1 release | A point dragged on the drawing, a note played | Whatever you drew. |

## In Serum
ENV 1 controls level in Serum too, with ATK, HOLD, DEC, SUS and REL knobs. From Init, raise ATK and REL and play. The “The amp envelope” Serum task checks a saved preset.

In the guide: envelopes, from p. 183; the envelope display on p. 186 shows times in ms or s and sustain in dB.

## Recap
- Attack, decay and release are times; sustain is a level.
- Sustain only matters while the key is held; release only after it is let go.
- A note with no sustain is a pluck; a note with full sustain is an organ.

## Guide notes
Read the four values back to the learner. Common mistake: expecting sustain to be a time. Hold is not used in this chapter; notes are struck and let go.
