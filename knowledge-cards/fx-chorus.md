## What it is
Chorus mixes the sound with one or more copies of itself that are delayed by a tiny, constantly changing amount.

## Why it works
Changing a delay time bends the pitch of the copy slightly up and down. The copy therefore drifts against the original the way two players never stay perfectly together, and the result sounds like several sources instead of one. Sending the copies to different sides gives width.

## Listen for
A gentle shimmer and widening on held notes. Higher depth or rate turns it into an obvious wobble or seasick warble.

## Goes with
Chorus overlaps with oscillator unison; both together can be too blurry. It tends to thin the centre of the stereo image, so basses often use little or none.
