## What it is
A second filter can sit after the first, so it works on what the first let through, or beside it, so both work on the original sound and their results are added.

## Why it works
In a row, each filter can only remove more, so what is left is whatever passes both: a low-pass followed by a high-pass leaves a band between their cutoffs. Side by side, the outputs are added, so what is heard is whatever passes either: the same two filters set wide apart leave the bottom and the top with a hole in the middle. The filters are the same in both cases; only the wiring changes the result.

## Listen for
In a row, the sound closing in as either cutoff moves toward the other. Side by side, the opposite: a region dropping out between the two while both ends stay.

## Goes with
Different filter types on each, such as a low-pass for the body with a peak or a band-pass added beside it for a moving emphasis. Moving the two cutoffs together or apart from one control.
