# Genre list for track L (genre studies)

Each genre uses the eight-lesson template in `docs/CURRICULUM.md`. One-line descriptions are working summaries,
to be checked against the library before any lesson is written. Related genres will share drum, bass and
technique lessons and differ mainly in their "signature" lessons.

## Selected by the owner (63)
Everything from the first list except future garage.

**Around glitch-hop:** glitch-hop, neurohop, ghetto funk, midtempo bass, wonky, LA beat scene / future beats,
purple sound, wave, halftime, experimental / freeform bass, IDM, glitch (clicks and cuts), braindance,
drill 'n' bass, breakcore, footwork / juke, trip-hop, illbient, folktronica, electro swing.

**Around breakbeat:** breakbeat, nu skool breaks, big beat, Florida breaks, progressive breaks, psybreaks,
tech-funk, acid breaks, electro, broken beat, breakbeat hardcore, jungle, drumfunk, neurofunk, techstep,
liquid drum and bass, autonomic, UK garage / 2-step, breakstep, UK bass, Jersey club.

**Around psydub:** psydub, psybient, psychill, ambient dub, dub, steppers dub, digidub, dub techno, deep dubstep,
psystep, chillstep, global bass, temple / sacred bass, organic downtempo, chillgressive, Goa trance,
progressive psytrance, Zenonesque, forest psytrance, Suomisaundi, Berlin school, space ambient / drone.

**Excluded by the owner:** future garage.

## Selected from the second list (16)
- **Bass and garage lineage:** dubstep (early UK), trap (electronic), bassline, speed garage, UK funky,
  Miami bass, Baltimore club.
- **Drum and bass and jungle:** minimal / deep drum and bass, atmospheric drum and bass, ragga jungle, sambass.
- **House:** organic house only. (The owner wants no other house or techno styles.)
- **Psychedelic:** darkpsy. (Progressive psytrance was already selected.)
- **Ambient and experimental:** ambient, lowercase / microsound.
- **Other:** nu jazz, downtempo / chillout.

## Selected from the third list (13)
Nu-funk, funky breaks, electro-funk / boogie, boom bap, turntablism, electro breaks, hardcore breaks,
jungle techno, footwork jungle, tribal ambient, dubtronica, digital dancehall, fourth world.

The owner has said the genre list is now closed: 92 genres in total (63 + 16 + 13). Everything else offered in
the third list was declined.

## Declined by the owner
Riddim, brostep / tearout, melodic dubstep, drumstep, future bass, hybrid trap, phonk, grime, weightless,
ghettotech, moombahton, deconstructed club; jump-up, dancefloor, darkstep and crossbreed drum and bass; all
global club styles (gqom, amapiano, kuduro, baile funk, Afro house, tribal house); all house and techno styles
other than organic house; full-on, hi-tech, psycore and Nitzhonot psytrance; progressive, tech and uplifting
trance; all hard and fast styles; dark ambient, musique concrete, acousmatic, plunderphonics, noise, industrial,
EBM, witch house, kosmische, generative ambient; synthwave, vaporwave, chillwave, lo-fi hip-hop, chiptune,
synth-pop, Italo disco, electroclash. Also future garage, from the first list.

**Settled with the owner (13 Nov 2026):** Goa trance, Zenonesque, forest psytrance, Suomisaundi and psybreaks
stay in. The owner's test for a psychedelic genre is whether it is "heady"; when in doubt, keep the heady ones.
Progressive, tech and uplifting trance stay out. **The owner's reason for the whole project is Dave Tipper:**
his kind of sound design (glitch-hop, psychedelic bass, intricate texture and movement) is the north star when
choosing what to teach first and which examples to use. Never claim to know how he made a specific sound.
