## You will be able to
Choose a wavetable and use its position control to move between shapes.

## Hear it first
A held note while the position sweeps from one end of a table to the other.

## The idea
A wavetable is a row of single-cycle shapes. The oscillator plays one at a time, and Position chooses which. Between neighbours the shapes blend, so sweeping the position changes the harmonics smoothly while the pitch stays put. That is something a single fixed shape cannot do.

## Steps
| Do | On screen | Check | You should hear |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open the table picker and choose "Basic morph". | Table picker, a view of the frames | Table selected | Depends on the frame |
| Hold a note and sweep Position end to end. | Position, spectrum | Position travelled the full range | Colour changing, pitch constant |
| Stop on a frame you like. | Same | Position moved from the default | Your chosen colour |
| Choose a second table and sweep again. | Same | A different table, position swept | A different kind of change |
| Find the brightest frame in this table. | Same | Position within the brightest region | The buzziest colour |

## Ear check
Two held notes: is the position moving or still? Then: which of two frames is brighter?

## In Serum
From Init, load any other wavetable into Osc A and move its position. Save and drop it: the app checks that the table and the position both changed.

In the guide: Wavetable Position, p. 45. The control is labelled WT POS.

## Recap
- A wavetable is a row of shapes.
- Position picks one and blends between neighbours.
- Sweeping it changes colour without changing pitch.

## Guide notes
The practice synth's tables are generated for this app and will not match Serum's. Say which direction of the sweep got brighter for the learner's chosen table.
