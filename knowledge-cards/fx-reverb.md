## What it is
Reverb imitates the sound of a room: thousands of reflections arriving so close together that they blur into a tail that fades away.

## Why it works
In a real space sound reaches you directly and then again off every surface, each reflection slightly later and quieter. The ear uses that tail to judge how big and how far away something is. A longer or larger setting suggests a bigger space; a higher mix moves the sound further from the listener; damping the high frequencies imitates soft surfaces and makes the space feel warmer.

## Listen for
Stop playing and listen to what remains. A short tail adds body; a long one becomes a wash that notes melt into.

## Goes with
Reverb blurs timing and muddies low frequencies, so it is usually kept modest on basses and placed after delay and distortion. A pre-delay setting leaves a gap before the tail starts, which keeps the dry note clear.
