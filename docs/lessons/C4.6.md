## You will be able to
Draw your own LFO shape, and use it as a repeating pattern in time with the beat.

## Hear it first
A wobble with a plain sine shape, then with a drawn pattern of steps.

## The idea
The four basic shapes are only a start. A drawn shape is sixteen steps, each at a height you choose. The LFO plays through them in order and starts again, so what you draw is a repeating pattern. Here the LFO is synced to four beats, so each of the sixteen steps lasts a quarter of a beat. That makes the drawing a rhythm: tall bars are moments when the filter is open, short ones when it is closed. One held note becomes a moving bassline of brightness.

## Steps
| Do | On screen | Check | You should hear |
|---|---|---|---|
| Switch on Hold and play a low note. LFO 1 is a slow sine, once per bar, with the click on. | LFO 1 shape, Filter curve | Sine shape, note held | A smooth sweep that takes a whole bar. |
| Change the Shape to Drawn. | LFO 1 shape, Filter curve | Drawn shape selected | A staircase: the filter stepping down through the bar, two clicks of the sixteen at a time. |
| Drag across the bars to draw a pattern of your own. Change at least six of them. | LFO 1 shape, Filter curve | At least six bars changed by dragging | The filter jumping to your pattern, repeating every bar in time with the click. |
| Make a rhythm: push four or more bars right down to the bottom and leave at least four at the top. | LFO 1 shape, Filter curve | Four or more bars at the bottom and four or more at the top | Bright bursts with dark gaps between them: the held note now has a rhythm. |
| Set “Repeats every” to 2 beats. | LFO 1 repeats every, Filter curve | Repeating every 2 beats | The same pattern, twice as fast. |

## Ear check
None.

## In Serum
Serum’s LFO display is a drawing surface: click to add points, drag to bend lines, and set a grid so points snap to divisions of the cycle (guide, LFO section from p. 187). It draws smooth curves as well as steps.

In the guide: drawing in the LFO display, LFO section from p. 187.

## Recap
- A drawn shape is a row of steps the LFO plays in order.
- Synced to the beat, the steps become a rhythm.
- One held note with a drawn LFO on the filter is already a pattern.

## Guide notes
Sixteen steps snapped to eighths of the height; steps only, no curves. Synced to four beats, each step is a quarter of a beat. The remark prints the learner's pattern as a row of x and dots.
