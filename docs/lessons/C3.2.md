## You will be able to
Set up the three classic loudness shapes from the same four controls, and match an envelope by ear.

## Hear it first
One note as an organ, a pluck and a pad.

## The idea
Most sounds fall into one of three loudness shapes. An organ switches on and off: no attack, full sustain, no release. A pluck starts at once and dies away by itself: no sustain, and the decay sets its length. A pad swells in and fades out: long attack, high sustain, long release. The oscillators can stay exactly the same. The shape in time is often what tells you what kind of instrument you are hearing.

## Steps
| Do | On screen | Check | You should hear |
|---|---|---|---|
| The synth starts as an organ. Play a few notes, short and long. | Envelope 1 drawing, Env 1 attack, Env 1 decay, Env 1 sustain, Env 1 release | Attack 10 ms or less, sustain 100%, release 50 ms or less, two notes played | On and off at once, at full level in between. |
| Make it a pluck: Sustain to 0, Decay somewhere between a fifth and half a second, and a little Release. | Envelope 1 drawing, Env 1 attack, Env 1 decay, Env 1 sustain, Env 1 release | Attack 10 ms or less, sustain 10% or less, decay 150 to 600 ms, release 100 to 600 ms, two notes played | A short note that dies away however long you hold the key. |
| Make it a pad: a long Attack, Sustain up high, and a long Release. Hold a key, then let go. | Envelope 1 drawing, Env 1 attack, Env 1 decay, Env 1 sustain, Env 1 release | Attack 400 ms or more, sustain 60% or more, release 800 ms or more, a note played | A swell in, a steady level, and a slow fade. |

## Ear check
Three match-by-ear rounds are built in: attack, then decay with sustain, then release.

## In Serum
From Init, make a pluck on ENV 1: SUS all the way down and DEC short. How ENV 1’s values read on screen is not confirmed yet, so nothing is checked here.

In the guide: envelopes, from p. 183.

## Recap
- Organ: instant on and off, full sustain.
- Pluck: instant start, no sustain; decay sets the length.
- Pad: slow attack, high sustain, slow release.

## Guide notes
Tell the learner how long their pluck lasts. Times match within double or half.
