## You will be able to
Turn resonance up until the filter itself sounds like a note, and play that note from the keyboard.

## Hear it first
Plain hiss, then the same hiss through a filter with its resonance right up: a whistle appears.

## The idea
At high resonance a filter rings at its cutoff, the way a glass rings when tapped. Feed it noise, which has energy at every frequency, and the ringing never stops: you hear a whistle with a clear pitch. On many synths, resonance at maximum makes the filter sing with nothing going in at all. That is called self-oscillation. This practice synth’s filter stops just short of it, so here it needs the noise to keep it going. The pitch still follows the cutoff, and with full key tracking you can play it.

## Steps
| Do | On screen | Check | You should hear |
|---|---|---|---|
| Switch on Hold and play a note. Oscillator A is turned right down, so you hear only noise through the filter. | Resonance, Filter curve, Spectrum | Resonance at 0, note held | A dull hiss with no pitch. |
| Raise Resonance all the way. | Resonance, Filter curve, Spectrum | Resonance at 95% or more | A wavering whistle rising out of the hiss. |
| Move the cutoff and listen to the whistle. | Cutoff, Filter curve, Spectrum | Cutoff moved by at least an octave | The whistle’s pitch following the cutoff. |
| Set Key track to 100% and play at least three different keys. | Key track, Filter curve | Key track at 100%, three different keys played | The whistle moving in step with the keys: a tune made of filtered noise. |
| Set the cutoff to about 262 Hz, the pitch of middle C. | Cutoff, Key track, Filter curve | Cutoff between 255 and 270 Hz, key track at 100% | The whistle now in tune with the keys. |
| Bring A level back up and play a few keys. | A level, Filter curve, Spectrum | A level at -18 dB or above, a key played | The oscillator and the whistle together, the peak sitting on the note’s lowest harmonic. |

## Recap
- High resonance makes the filter ring at its cutoff.
- The cutoff frequency is the pitch of that ring.
- With full key tracking the filter can be played like an oscillator.
