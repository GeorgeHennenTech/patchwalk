## What it is
Mono makes the synth play only one note at a time. Legato stops the envelopes from restarting when you press a new key before releasing the last. Portamento makes the pitch slide from the old note to the new one instead of jumping.

## Why it works
Together these make the synth behave like a single voice or a wind instrument rather than a keyboard. With only one note sounding, overlapping keys become a way of phrasing: play detached and each note gets a fresh attack, play overlapped and the notes join into one line. The slide between pitches fills in the gap the ear would otherwise hear as a jump, and its time sets whether that sounds like a quick scoop or a long swoop.

## Listen for
Overlap two notes and the second should arrive without a new attack, sliding up or down from the first. Play them separately and each starts cleanly.

## Goes with
Because legato skips the attack, filter and amp envelope plucks are only heard on the first note of a run. Basses and leads use this combination far more often than pads or chords.
