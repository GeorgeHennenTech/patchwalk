## What it is
The mod wheel is not routed straight to a sound setting. It turns a macro, and that macro in turn drives or scales something else, often the depth of an LFO or envelope route.

## Why it works
Routing through a macro gives the same control two handles: the wheel for live playing and the macro knob for automation or for saving a starting position. When the macro is used to scale another route, it acts as a depth control. At rest the movement is absent or subtle; pushing the wheel lets more of it through. That keeps a patch playable, because the player decides when the movement appears.

## Listen for
With the wheel down the sound should be steadier. Push it up and the movement that the scaled route creates should grow in proportion.

## Goes with
This pairs naturally with vibrato, filter wobble and other movement that would be tiring if it were always on. If the wheel seems to do nothing, check the macro's own routes and their amounts.
