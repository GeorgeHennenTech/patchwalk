## You will be able to
Describe any modulation as a source, a destination and an amount, and stack several sources on one knob.

## Hear it first
One filter knob moved by one source, then by two at once, then by three.

## The idea
Every connection you have made so far has the same three parts. A source is something that moves: an envelope or an LFO. A destination is the knob it moves. The amount is how far. Together they are called a route. The knob itself still matters: it sets where the movement is centred. And one knob can have several routes at once. Their movements simply add together, which is how complicated motion is built from simple parts.

## Steps
| Do | On screen | Check | You should hear |
|---|---|---|---|
| Switch on Hold and play a low note. Send LFO 1 to Filter cutoff. | LFO 1 sends to, LFO 1 amount, Cutoff, Filter curve | LFO 1 sent to Filter cutoff, note held | A wobble. |
| Turn LFO 1’s Amount to exactly 0. Double-click resets a knob; here use the arrow keys. | LFO 1 amount, Cutoff, Filter curve | Amount at 0, still sent to Filter cutoff | Nothing moving. The route still exists, but an amount of zero does nothing, and the ring on Cutoff has gone. |
| Bring Amount up to about 40%. | LFO 1 amount, Cutoff, Filter curve | Amount between 30% and 50% | The wobble returning, as deep as you make it. |
| Turn the Cutoff knob up and down while it wobbles. | Cutoff, Filter curve | Cutoff moved across at least an octave | The same movement, shifted brighter or darker. The knob is the centre; the route adds to it. |
| Send LFO 3 to Filter cutoff as well. | LFO 3 sends to, LFO 3 amount, LFO 3 rate, Cutoff, Filter curve | LFO 1 and LFO 3 both sent to Filter cutoff | The fast wobble now riding up and down on a slow sweep. Cutoff wears two rings: cyan and orange. |
| Send Envelope 2 to Filter cutoff too, then strike a new key. | Env 2 sends to, Env 2 amount, Cutoff, Filter curve | Envelope 2, LFO 1 and LFO 3 all sent to Filter cutoff, a note played | Each new note also starts bright and settles. Three movements, one knob. |

## In Serum
Serum lists every route on its MATRIX tab: source, destination and amount in one row each. Dragging a source onto a knob adds a row there. Lessons on the matrix itself come later.

In the guide: the Mod Matrix (see the matrix chapter).

## Recap
- A route is a source, a destination and an amount.
- The knob sets the centre; routes add movement to it.
- Several routes on one knob add together.

## Guide notes
List the learner's routes back to them as source, destination, amount. An amount of zero leaves the route in place but draws no ring. Serum's MATRIX tab itself is a later lesson.
