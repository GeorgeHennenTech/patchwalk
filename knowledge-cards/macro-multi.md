## What it is
A single macro knob is routed to more than one destination, so turning it changes several settings together, each by its own amount.

## Why it works
Many changes in a sound only make sense as a group: opening a filter while adding drive, or widening the stereo image while raising an effect. Tying them to one control keeps them in proportion and lets you perform a complex change as one gesture. Each route has its own amount and direction, so the destinations do not have to move equally or even the same way.

## Listen for
Sweep the macro slowly and notice that the sound changes character, not just one quality. Then mute the routes one at a time in your own copy to hear what each contributes.

## Goes with
Macros are the natural target for automation in a track and for a mod wheel or controller knob. A macro that does several jobs is best named for the result you hear, not for any one destination.
