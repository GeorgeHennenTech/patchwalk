## You will be able to
Send one source to another source’s settings, so the movement itself changes over time.

## Hear it first
A steady wobble, then a wobble whose speed is itself being moved.

## The idea
A destination does not have to be part of the sound. It can be a setting of another source. Send a slow LFO to a fast LFO’s rate and the wobble speeds up and slows down by itself. Send an envelope there and every note’s wobble starts fast and settles. This is how sounds are made to feel alive: the movement is never quite the same twice. It is also where patches get hard to follow, so add one layer at a time.

## Steps
| Do | On screen | Check | You should hear |
|---|---|---|---|
| Switch on Hold and play a low note. LFO 1 is already wobbling the filter at a steady speed. | LFO 1 rate, LFO 2 sends to, Filter curve | LFO 2 sent to nothing, note held | A steady “wub wub wub”. |
| Send LFO 2 to LFO 1 rate. Slow LFO 2’s own Rate right down to about 0.3 and raise its Amount to 60% or more. | LFO 2 sends to, LFO 2 rate, LFO 2 amount, LFO 1 rate, Filter curve | LFO 2 sent to LFO 1 rate, its rate 0.1 to 0.5 Hz, its amount 60% or more | The wobble speeding up and slowing down in a slow cycle. LFO 1’s Rate knob wears a green ring. |
| Change LFO 2’s Shape to Square. | LFO 2 shape, LFO 2 rate, LFO 1 rate | LFO 2 on Square | Two speeds taking turns: slow wobble, fast wobble, slow, fast. |
| Set LFO 2’s “Sends to” back to Nothing. Send Envelope 2 to LFO 1 rate instead, with a long Decay, and strike a new key. | LFO 2 sends to, Env 2 sends to, Env 2 amount, Env 2 decay, LFO 1 rate | LFO 2 sent to nothing, envelope 2 sent to LFO 1 rate, decay 1 s or more, a note played | Each note’s wobble starting fast and slowing to its normal speed. |

## In Serum
In Serum you can drag one LFO onto another LFO’s RATE knob, or onto a route’s amount. The app’s rebuilt-patch view reports these as “modulated modulators” when it finds them in a preset.

In the guide: LFOs, from p. 187 (RATE as a destination).

## Recap
- A source’s own settings can be destinations.
- A slow LFO on a fast LFO’s rate makes the movement itself move.
- An envelope there gives every note its own change of speed.
