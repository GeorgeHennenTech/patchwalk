## What it is
Macros are a small set of assignable knobs. On their own they do nothing; each is given a name and routed to one or more settings, and from then on it is a front-panel control for that part of the sound.

## Why it works
A finished patch may have dozens of settings, but only a few are worth changing while playing. Macros gather those into labelled controls, so the patch can be performed and automated without opening its inner pages. The saved position of each macro is part of the sound, because a macro adds to whatever it is routed to.

## Listen for
Turn each macro through its range and note what changes. A well-made macro does something musical across the whole sweep.

## Goes with
Macros are usually what you would map to a hardware knob or draw automation for. A macro's name describes the intended result, which is a useful clue when reading someone else's patch.
