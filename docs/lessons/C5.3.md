## You will be able to
Use how hard a key is struck, and which key it is, as sources.

## Hear it first
A soft note and a hard note that sound the same, then the same two with velocity sent to the filter.

## The idea
Envelopes and LFOs move by themselves. Two other sources come from your hands. Velocity is how hard a key is struck, and Key position is how high or low the key is. Each is fixed for the length of a note: one number, decided the moment the note starts. On a real instrument, playing harder is louder and also brighter, and high notes behave differently from low ones. Sending these two sources to the right knobs is what makes a synth respond to playing. Here, click lower on a key to strike harder, or use C and V to set how hard the letter keys strike.

## Steps
| Do | On screen | Check | You should hear |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strike a key softly and then hard: click near the top of a key, then near its bottom. | Velocity sends to | Velocity sent to nothing; one note at 60 or softer and one at 100 or harder | No difference at all. Nothing is listening to how hard you play. |
| Send Velocity to Note level, then strike soft and hard again. | Velocity sends to, Velocity amount | Velocity sent to Note level; a soft note and a hard note played | Soft notes quiet, hard notes loud. |
| Now send Velocity to Filter cutoff instead, and strike soft and hard. | Velocity sends to, Velocity amount, Cutoff, Filter curve | Velocity sent to Filter cutoff; a soft note and a hard note played | Soft notes dull, hard notes bright, at the same loudness. Cutoff wears a red ring. |
| Send Key position to Filter cutoff as well, and play low and high keys. Z and X move the letter keys by octaves. | Key position sends to, Key position amount, Cutoff, Filter curve | Key position sent to Filter cutoff; three different keys played | High notes brighter than low ones. This is key tracking, built from a route. |
| Send Key position to Note level instead and turn its Amount below zero. Play low and high keys. | Key position sends to, Key position amount | Key position sent to Note level, amount below zero; two different keys played | High notes quieter than low ones: a way to stop a bright sound getting piercing at the top. |

## Recap
- Velocity is how hard a note is struck; key position is which key it is.
- Both are fixed for the length of a note.
- Sent to level and brightness, they make a synth respond to playing.

## Guide notes
Velocity comes from where a key is clicked (lower is harder) or the C and V keys. Tell the learner their softest and hardest note. Only one destination per source, which the lesson uses to motivate a matrix.
