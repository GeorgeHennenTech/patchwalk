## What it is
An LFO is routed to a pitch setting such as fine tune, semitones, octave or master tuning, so the pitch moves in a repeating pattern.

## Why it works
A small, smooth pitch movement is vibrato, the same wavering a singer or string player adds to a held note; it makes a static tone feel alive. A larger or stepped movement stops sounding like expression and becomes a pattern: a square shape routed to octave or semitones makes the note jump between two pitches like a trill or a tiny arpeggio. The route amount decides which of those you get.

## Listen for
With a small amount, a gentle waver around the note. With a large amount or a stepped shape, clear jumps between pitches at the LFO's rate.

## Goes with
Vibrato usually sounds most natural when it fades in after the note starts, which is what an LFO rise or delay setting, or a mod wheel, is for.
