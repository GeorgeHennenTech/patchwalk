# Course plan: guided lessons with a built-in practice synth

Status: **plan only, nothing here is built.** Written for the owner to review and reorder.

## What changes
Today the Learn tab has ten lessons of fixed text, each ending in a task you do in Serum and check by dropping
the saved preset. This plan replaces that with a full course in which every lesson is a guided walk-through on
a synth built into the page, so you hear each idea the moment you touch a control.

This widens the project's scope: `CLAUDE.md` currently says "no audio". The practice synth makes sound in the
browser. It still never records, uploads or analyses audio from Serum.

## The practice synth
A small synthesizer written for this app with the browser's Web Audio API. It is a teaching instrument, not a
copy of Serum: it will not sound identical and must not look like it (rule 4). Its value is that every control
is real, labelled in real units, and visible to the lesson.

| Part | What it has | First needed in |
|---|---|---|
| Oscillators | Two oscillators: sine, triangle, saw, square, and a few morphing wavetables generated by maths (no Xfer tables). Octave, semitone, fine, level, pan, phase. | Chapter 1 |
| Unison | Voices, detune, stereo spread. | Chapter 1 |
| Sub and noise | Sine sub one or two octaves down; white and pink noise. | Chapter 1 |
| Filter | Low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, notch; cutoff, resonance, drive, key tracking; two slopes. | Chapter 2 |
| Envelopes | Amp envelope and two assignable envelopes, with attack, hold, decay, sustain, release and curve. | Chapter 3 |
| LFOs | Two LFOs: shape, rate in Hz or tempo-synced, rise, trigger mode. | Chapter 4 |
| Mod matrix | Eight routes: source, destination, amount, bipolar, scaled-by. Four macros, mod wheel, velocity, key tracking. | Chapter 5 |
| Warp and FM | A handful of warp modes (bend, sync, mirror, phase distortion) and oscillator B modulating A. | Chapter 6 |
| Effects | Distortion, EQ, compressor, chorus, delay, reverb, in a reorderable rack. | Chapter 7 |
| Voicing | Poly or mono, legato, glide, pitch-bend range. | Chapter 8 |
| Playing | On-screen keyboard, computer keys, a "hold note" button, optional MIDI keyboard, and a simple looping pattern so you can listen hands-free. | Chapter 0 |
| Seeing | Oscilloscope (wave shape), spectrum (harmonics), and live drawings of the envelope, LFO and filter curve. | Chapter 0 |

Because it is our own synth, its readings are exact. Lessons can say "set cutoff to 800 Hz" and check it, which
the Serum side cannot do until the probe presets exist.

## How a lesson works
Every lesson has the same six parts.

1. **Hear it first.** One button plays a before and an after, so you know what you are about to learn.
2. **The idea.** Two or three sentences plus the relevant knowledge card.
3. **Guided steps.** One instruction at a time. Only the controls that step needs are on screen (see below).
   Each step has a target that is checked live as you move the control, with a tick the moment you hit it.
4. **Ear check.** Two or three short listening questions ("which one has more resonance?") with the controls
   hidden, so you learn the sound and not just the knob position.
5. **Now in Serum (optional).** The same task in Serum, checked by dropping the saved preset as today.
6. **Recap and reading.** Three lines of what you learned and the card's further-reading links.

### How controls appear
- **Spotlight.** A step shows only its own controls, large, next to the matching picture: one knob and a scope
  for "octave"; four sliders and an envelope drawing for "ADSR".
- **Accumulating panel.** Controls you have already learned stay available in a smaller strip, so the synth
  grows as the course goes on. Chapter 1 has an oscillator; by chapter 7 it is the whole instrument.
- **Full synth drawer.** A button opens everything at once for free play. Controls not yet taught are dimmed
  with the name of the lesson that covers them.
- **Reset and compare.** Each step can snap back to its starting state, and toggle between "start" and "now".

## Checking your work
- **On the practice synth:** targets are checked live against the synth's own state, with exact values and
  tolerances chosen per step ("within a semitone", "between 400 and 1200 Hz"). No file needed.
- **Ear checks:** the app plays two versions it generates itself and knows the answer.
- **In Serum:** unchanged from today. These checks read stored values only and stay limited to things the app
  reads reliably until probe presets confirm more.

## What the teacher does
Not part of this plan's first pass, but designed in: each step has an "ask" slot. When it is added, the teacher
can see the lesson, the step, and the current state of the practice synth, so its answers can refer to what you
actually have dialled in. Whether it may use general synthesis knowledge for concept questions is still an open
decision for the owner.

## Build order
Each stage is demoable on its own and replaces nothing until it is ready.

| Stage | Delivers | You can then |
|---|---|---|
| A | Synth engine with one oscillator, filter, amp envelope; keyboard; scope and spectrum | Play notes and see the wave |
| B | Lesson player: spotlight controls, live targets, reset/compare, progress | Take chapters 0 and 1 |
| C | Envelope drawing, second envelope, first mod routes | Take chapters 2 and 3 |
| D | LFOs, mod matrix, macros, tempo | Take chapters 4 and 5 |
| E | Effects rack, voicing | Take chapters 7 and 8 |
| F | Warp and FM | Take chapter 6 |
| G | Ear checks across all chapters; recipes | Take chapter 9 |
| H | Chapter 10; "open this step on the practice synth" from a rebuilt patch, for settings the synth has | Finish the course |

The current ten lessons stay as they are until stage B, then become the "Now in Serum" tasks of the matching
new lessons.

## Limits to be honest about
- The practice synth will not sound like Serum. Ideas transfer; exact tones do not.
- Browsers only start audio after a click, add a little delay between key and sound, and vary on phones.
- Audio cannot be judged by automated tests. The synth's settings logic and every lesson's targets will be
  tested; how it sounds needs your ears at each stage.
- 62 lessons need 62 sets of steps and roughly 25 more knowledge cards. I would draft them; they stay marked
  "draft" until you have reviewed them.
- This is the largest piece of work in the project so far, several times the size of the current Learn tab.
