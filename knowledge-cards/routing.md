## What it is
Each sound source can be sent through a filter or straight on to the effects and output. Routing decides which.

## Why it works
A filter changes everything that passes through it, which is not always wanted. Sending a sub around the filter keeps the low end solid while the filter sweeps the rest. Sending only one oscillator through it lets a filtered layer and an unfiltered layer coexist. Routing therefore decides which parts of a patch move together and which stay fixed.

## Listen for
Sweep the filter and notice what does not change. Anything that stays constant is not going through it.

## Goes with
Routing choices explain why a filter sweep can sound thin in one patch and full in another with the same filter settings.
