## You will be able to
Send a second envelope to the filter’s cutoff so each note changes brightness as it sounds.

## Hear it first
A dull filtered note, then the same note with an envelope opening the filter at its start.

## The idea
An envelope is only a shape in time. Envelope 1 is wired to loudness, but Envelope 2 can be sent to other controls. Sent to the cutoff, it pushes the cutoff up at the start of each note and lets it fall back: a bright start that closes down. That is the sound of most synth basses and plucks. Amount sets how far the envelope pushes. The cutoff knob stays where you set it; it grows a yellow ring showing the stretch the envelope moves it across.

## Steps
| Do | On screen | Check | You should hear |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strike a key. The filter is on and set dark, and Envelope 2 is not connected. | Env 2 sends to, Cutoff | Envelope 2 sent to nothing, a note played | A dull, steady note. |
| Set Envelope 2’s “Sends to” to Filter cutoff, and strike a few keys. | Env 2 sends to, Env 2 amount, Cutoff, Envelope 2 drawing | Envelope 2 sent to Filter cutoff, two notes played | Each note starting brighter and closing down. Look for the yellow ring on Cutoff. |
| Raise Amount to about 80%. | Env 2 amount, Cutoff, Envelope 2 drawing | Amount between 70% and 100%, a note played | A much brighter start: the envelope is pushing the cutoff further. |
| Shorten Envelope 2’s Decay to about 100 ms. | Env 2 decay, Env 2 amount, Cutoff, Envelope 2 drawing | Decay between 60 and 160 ms, a note played | A tight click of brightness at the front of each note. |
| Now lengthen it to about a second and a half, and hold a key. | Env 2 decay, Env 2 amount, Cutoff, Envelope 2 drawing | Decay between 1 and 2.5 s, a note played | A slow “wow” as the filter closes. |
| Raise the Cutoff knob to about 2 kHz and turn Amount below zero. | Env 2 amount, Cutoff, Envelope 2 drawing | Cutoff at 1.5 kHz or above, amount below zero, a note played | The other way round: each note starts muffled and opens up. |

## In Serum
In Serum, drag ENV 2 onto Filter 1’s CUTOFF knob to connect it; a ring on the knob shows the range. Save it and drop it into the “LFOs and modulation” Serum task to see what the app can read from it.

In the guide: assigning modulation by dragging a source onto a knob (see the modulation chapter); filter CUTOFF, p. 141.

## Recap
- A second envelope can be sent to the cutoff.
- Amount sets how far it pushes; decay sets how long the change takes.
- A negative amount turns the shape upside down.

## Guide notes
Point at the yellow ring on Cutoff. The practice synth uses a menu to connect; Serum uses drag and drop. One destination per source here.
