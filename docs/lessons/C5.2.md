## You will be able to
Choose whether a source pushes a control both ways from its knob, or one way only.

## Hear it first
A vibrato that wavers around the note, then one that only goes above it.

## The idea
An LFO normally swings both ways: above the knob’s setting and below it by the same distance. That is called bipolar. Set to one way, it only moves between the knob’s setting and one side of it. That is called unipolar. On pitch the difference is easy to hear. Both ways, the note wavers around its true pitch and still sounds in tune. One way, it only ever goes sharp, so the whole note sounds raised. An envelope is always one way: it starts from the knob and pushes in one direction.

## Steps
| Do | On screen | Check | You should hear |
|---|---|---|---|
| Switch on Hold and play a note. LFO 1 is on Osc A pitch, moving both ways. | LFO 1 direction, LFO 1 amount, Fine | Both ways, note held | A wide vibrato centred on the note. |
| Switch LFO 1’s Direction to One way. | LFO 1 direction, LFO 1 amount, Fine | One way selected | The waver now sits above the note, so it sounds sharp. The ring on Fine now reaches one side only. |
| Change the Shape to Square, slow the Rate to about 2, and set Amount to 17%. | LFO 1 shape, LFO 1 rate, LFO 1 amount, LFO 1 direction | Square, one way, rate 1 to 3 Hz, amount between 15% and 19% | A trill: your note, then the note a whole tone above, back and forth. |
| Switch back to Both ways and listen to the same trill. | LFO 1 direction | Both ways selected | Now it jumps between a note below and a note above. The key you are holding is never heard. |
| Go back to One way and turn Amount below zero. | LFO 1 direction, LFO 1 amount | One way, amount below zero | A trill downward: your note and a note below it. |

## In Serum
On Serum’s MATRIX tab each route has a direction switch that chooses between one way and both ways. Its exact labels are not confirmed here, so nothing is checked.

In the guide: the Mod Matrix (route direction).

## Recap
- Both ways (bipolar) swings either side of the knob.
- One way (unipolar) moves from the knob to one side only.
- With one way, the knob’s own setting is always one end of the movement.

## Guide notes
Pitch makes the difference obvious. The trill step relies on LFO shapes reaching exactly full depth, which the engine now guarantees. The practice synth's switch is per LFO; in Serum direction belongs to each route.
