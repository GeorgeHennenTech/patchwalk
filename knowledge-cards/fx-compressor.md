## What it is
A compressor turns the level down automatically when the signal gets loud, then the whole thing is turned back up. The gap between the loudest and quietest moments gets smaller.

## Why it works
Taming the peaks lets the average level rise, so a sound seems denser and more present without its peaks getting higher. How quickly the compressor reacts shapes the start of each note: a slow reaction lets the initial hit through before clamping down, a fast one flattens it. A multiband compressor does this separately for low, middle and high ranges, which can make every part of the spectrum feel pushed forward at once.

## Listen for
A more even, solid level, with quiet details such as effect tails becoming more obvious. Too much sounds squashed or makes the level audibly pump.

## Goes with
Placed after distortion and before delay and reverb, it controls the tone; placed last, it also lifts the effect tails.
