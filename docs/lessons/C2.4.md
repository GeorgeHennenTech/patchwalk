## You will be able to
Hear the difference between a gentle and a steep filter, and pick one on purpose.

## Hear it first
The same cutoff twice: first a gentle slope, then a steep one.

## The idea
A filter does not cut everything past the cutoff at once. It turns things down more and more the further past the cutoff they are. Slope is how fast: it is measured in decibels per octave. With the gentle slope here, a harmonic one octave above the cutoff is 12 dB down. With the steep one it is 24 dB down. Steeper sounds darker and more decisive at the same cutoff.

## Steps
| Do | On screen | Check | You should hear |
|---|---|---|---|
| Switch on Hold and play a note on the gentle slope. | Slope, Filter curve | Gentle slope, note held | A darkened tone that still has some edge. |
| Switch to the steep slope. | Slope, Filter curve, Spectrum | Steep slope selected | Darker, though the cutoff has not moved. The curve falls twice as fast. |
| Switch back and forth a few times while the note sounds. | Slope, Filter curve, Spectrum | Switched at least four times | The upper harmonics stepping down and up. |
| On the steep slope, raise the cutoff until it is about as bright as the gentle slope was. | Slope, Cutoff, Filter curve | Steep slope, cutoff between 750 Hz and 3 kHz, moved in this step | Similar brightness, but with a cleaner edge to where the sound stops. |
| Add some resonance and sweep the cutoff on the steep slope. | Cutoff, Resonance, Filter curve | Steep slope, resonance 40% or more, cutoff swept across two octaves | A more dramatic sweep than the gentle slope gave. |

## In Serum
In Filter 1’s type menu, the number at the end of a type’s name is its slope in dB per octave (the manual lists 6, 12, 18 and 24). Compare a 12 and a 24 of the same family. Listening only.

In the guide: filter types, p. 135: the number in a type's name is its slope in dB per octave.

## Recap
- Slope is how fast the filter turns things down past the cutoff.
- It is counted in decibels per octave: 12 is gentle, 24 is steep.
- Steeper sounds darker at the same cutoff.

## Guide notes
Report the cutoff the learner needed on the steep slope to match the gentle one.
