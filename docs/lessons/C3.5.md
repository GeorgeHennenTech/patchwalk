## You will be able to
Send an envelope to pitch to make drops, zaps and scoops.

## Hear it first
A plain note, then a pitch drop at its start, then a very fast one.

## The idea
The same envelope can be sent to pitch. A note that starts high and falls quickly to its real pitch has a hard, percussive front: that is how synth kick drums and laser zaps are made. A note that starts a little low and rises sounds like a singer scooping up to it. Short and large reads as an impact. Long and small reads as expression.

## Steps
| Do | On screen | Check | You should hear |
|---|---|---|---|
| Set Envelope 2’s “Sends to” to Osc A pitch and strike a few keys. | Env 2 sends to, Env 2 amount, Envelope 2 drawing, Fine | Envelope 2 sent to Osc A pitch, two notes played | Each note dropping into place: “pyew”. |
| Raise Amount all the way and shorten Decay to about 60 ms. Play low keys. | Env 2 decay, Env 2 amount, Envelope 2 drawing | Amount at 90% or more, decay between 30 and 100 ms, a note played | A thump: the drop is too fast to hear as a slide. |
| Lengthen Decay to about 300 ms and set Amount a little below zero. | Env 2 decay, Env 2 amount, Envelope 2 drawing | Amount between -40% and -5%, decay between 150 and 500 ms, a note played | Each note scooping up to its pitch from below. |
| Switch on Osc B and send Envelope 2 to Osc B pitch instead, with Amount back above 50%. | Osc B, Env 2 sends to, Env 2 amount, Envelope 2 drawing | Osc B on, envelope 2 sent to Osc B pitch, amount 50% or more, a note played | A steady note from A with a falling one from B on top: a knock at the front of the sound. |

## In Serum
In Serum, drag ENV 2 onto an oscillator’s CRS (coarse pitch) control for a drop. Listening only: how the amount reads on screen is not confirmed.

In the guide: oscillator pitch controls, p. 40 (CRS is coarse pitch).

## Recap
- An envelope on pitch makes each note slide into place.
- Fast and far is an impact; slow and slight is a scoop.
- Sending it to one oscillator only adds a knock on top of a steady tone.
