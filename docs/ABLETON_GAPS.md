# What Ableton Live does that the Studio does not (17 Nov 2026)

Made by reading the Ableton Live 11 Reference Manual in the library (pages 49 to 690 and 811 to 856; the Push
hardware chapters and licensing were left out) against the Studio as it stood that day. Page numbers are the
manual's. Sizes are guesses: S = hours, M = a day, L = several days, XL = a week or more. Tick an item off
here when it is built. Everything built stays our own device or feature doing the same job.

## Lessons (owner, 17 Nov 2026)
The owner wants lessons on every one of these features: "this ableton course will be extensive". Nothing is
written yet and the lesson engine does not know the Studio. Plan it as its own course (how a DAW works, the
arrangement, clips and the piano roll, drums and the rack, mixing, each device, automation, recording,
sidechain, returns, exporting), each lesson done in the Studio and ending with the same thing in Ableton
Live, cited from the manual. Every feature ticked below needs a lesson.

## Suggested order

### 1. Quick wins (each S, together about two sessions)
- [x] Tap tempo (p.176)
- [x] Note tools in the piano roll: transpose, double and halve speed, reverse, invert, legato (p.172)
- [x] Duplicate loop: double a clip's length and contents (p.161, 200)
- [x] Scale mode: choose root and scale, in-scale rows highlighted, Fold to the key (p.163, 197-199). Plain Fold (rows with notes only) not built.
- [x] Note chance: a probability per note, in a lane under velocity (17 Nov); randomize velocity ("Random loud", 18 Nov) (p.205-209)
- [x] Hear a note as you click or move it (p.194)
- [x] Count-in when Record starts the song (17 Nov); a choice of none, 1, 2 or 4 bars, and an Overdub switch: off, recording replaces what the clip held (18 Nov) (p.290, 296)
- [x] Click a bar number to play from there; Shift+Space plays on from the bar where it stopped (p.115-116)
- [x] Locators: name a bar by right-clicking its number, click to play from it (17 Nov); jump to the one before and after (18 Nov) (p.117-119)
- [ ] Browser: search box, hear a drum or sample before loading it (DONE 17 Nov); hear a break or sound before loading (not yet) (p.76-78)
- [ ] Shortcuts: F1-F8 track on/off (DONE 17 Nov), Ctrl/Cmd+Up/Down halve or double the loop, Ctrl/Cmd+I insert silence,
      A show automation, H and W fit, Ctrl/Cmd+Alt+T return track, Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+E export MIDI (p.845-856)
- [x] Note Echo note effect (p.676)
- [x] Sidechain on Gate and Glue compressor (17 Nov); Auto filter and Multiband (18 Nov)
      (p.393, 442, 445, 467)
- [x] Utility: bass mono, left-only / right-only / swapped, DC filter; Saturator: soft clip and colour (18 Nov) (p.489, 509-510)
- [x] Effects on the master (p.281)

### 2. The biggest limits today (M to L each)
- [x] Grid sizes with triplets, notes off the sixteenth grid, Quantize (built 17 Nov; not yet: snap off, a quantize amount in the page, Ctrl/Cmd+1 to 4)
      (p.128, 200-203)
- [x] Clips of any length placed anywhere, split anywhere (built 18 Nov: clips start and end on any beat, Ctrl/Cmd+E cuts at the time marker; not yet: off the beat) (p.123, 128-130)
- [x] A sampler instrument: one sound played across the keys, held, looping and one-shot (built 17 Nov; not yet: a filter and LFO of its own, loop crossfade, warp, slicing) (Simpler, p.608-622)
- [x] Slice a sound onto pads in 4, 8 or 16 equal slices, with a clip that replays them (built 17 Nov; not yet: slicing at the hits themselves, 32 slices) (p.229-232, 613-614, 621)
- [ ] Knobs on the built-in drums: Decay and Tone as well as Tune (built 17 Nov); not yet: click and drive per drum, a filter and saturator per pad
      (p.558-560, 657-665)
- [x] Automation that moves with its clip, with a lock switch (p.365)
- [x] Automation: record knob moves, lanes on returns and the master, ready-made shapes, curved segments (Alt-drag), automated controls marked, and "Back to automation" after touching one (all built by 18 Nov; not yet: marks on the synth's own knobs) (p.353-365)
- [ ] Loop brace you drag, with numeric start and length; cut, duplicate and delete time (p.121-129)

### 3. Bigger pieces
- [ ] Device presets and hot-swap (p.312-314)
- [ ] Groove pool: timing, random and velocity amounts per clip, and commit (p.235-240)
- [ ] An LFO device and an envelope follower that move any knob on a track (p.667-669)
- [ ] Macro knobs over a track's chain, with randomize and stored variations; then parallel chains (p.332-350)
- [ ] Group tracks (p.278-280)
- [ ] MIDI map mode: click a control, move a hardware knob (p.684-686)
- [ ] Step recording and loop overdub; Capture what was just played (p.292-300)
- [ ] Session View: clip slots, scenes, quantized launching (p.136-148, 241-251). L to XL.
- [ ] Audio tracks: sound files on the timeline, played at their own speed or re-pitched to tempo
      (p.54-55, 178-189). XL; true time-stretching may not be feasible in a browser.
- [ ] Export: selected stretch only, single tracks (stems), normalize (p.86-89)
- [ ] A four-oscillator FM synth (Operator, p.561-582)

### 4. Deeper versions of devices we have
Compressor: RMS mode, sidechain EQ with listen, transfer-curve display (p.407-411). Reverb: size, freeze,
stereo, early reflections (p.484-487). Auto filter: 24 dB slope, drive, stepped synced cutoff (p.392-394).
Beat repeat: repeat-now button, pitch and pitch decay, filter (p.397). Delay: freeze and LFO (p.419). Echo:
separate left and right times, built-in reverb (p.424-428). Chorus: ensemble and vibrato (p.403-404). Phaser
and flanger: doubler, synced LFO, notches (p.475-476). Multiband: per-band solo and thresholds (p.465-466).
Arpeggiator: more styles, retrigger, velocity decay (p.518-521). Scale: editable map (p.525). Velocity:
clip, gate and fixed modes (p.526). Synth: a sound file as a wavetable, random-per-note source, split filter
routing, more unison modes (p.638-648).

### 5. Devices with no counterpart yet
Grain delay (M, p.446), convolution reverb with your own impulse file (S-M, p.450), vocoder (M-L, p.512),
Dynamic Tube (S, p.423), Corpus (L, p.412), Looper (L, needs audio in, p.457), Spectral Resonator and
Spectral Time (XL, p.495-503), Collision and Electric (L each, p.540-556), Sampler (XL, p.583), Tension (XL).

### Not possible or not worth it in a browser
Ableton Link (a page cannot join a local network session), external hardware effects and instruments,
cue/pre-listen outputs, MIDI timecode, video, MPE editing (the synth has no per-note inputs).
