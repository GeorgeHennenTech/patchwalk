## What it is
Two controls beside the keys that are moved by hand while a note sounds. The bend wheel pushes the pitch up or down and springs back to the middle when let go. The mod wheel stays where it is left and can be sent to any setting.

## Why it works
A note's settings are normally fixed by the patch, or moved by sources that run on their own, such as envelopes and LFOs. The wheels add the player to that list. The bend wheel always moves pitch, by a distance the patch sets as its bend range. The mod wheel is a source like any other: it has a destination and an amount, and its position decides how much of that amount is applied.

## Listen for
Bending should sound like a string being pushed: a smooth slide away from the note and back. With the mod wheel at the bottom the sound should be as the patch left it; as the wheel rises, whatever it is connected to should move in step with the hand.

## Goes with
A common use of the mod wheel is to bring in movement that would be tiring if it were always there, such as vibrato or a filter opening. Sending the wheel to the amount of another connection makes it a depth control for that movement.
